# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha
- First version: **Hangman**. Guess the WoW word by clicking the letters. Six attempts; a wrong letter takes one. **Hint** shows one letter and takes an attempt (once per round), **Give up** shows the word, **New round** picks another.
- Random words from eight categories (raid bosses, dungeons, zones and cities, classes and specs, races and factions, professions, items and gear, lore and NPCs), or **Anything**. A word you had lately is not picked again.
- **Own word:** one types a word (hidden) and an optional hint, and hands over the computer.
- Statistics (rounds, wins, win rate, streak, best streak) and the last ten words, saved per character.
- The Allemano look with lime as Arcade's colour; `/arcade` (or `/aa`) opens it.
- Connect Four, Rock Paper Scissors and Battleships are on the home page as "coming soon".
